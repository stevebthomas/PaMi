## 1. priya-heads-up-dm — "Hey, heads up, got a few support tickets overnight about checkout failures... Wanted to flag before standup."

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** high · **id:** `priya-heads-up-dm-ack-gap`

**Why it's ambiguous:** It is phrased as a pure FYI ("heads up", "wanted to flag"), contains no question and no ask, yet it is `requiresResponse: true` with a 30-minute deadline that gates `priya-early-followup` and the `lateResponseTo` tone nudge. A novice reads "not sure if it's a pattern yet" as Priya still investigating on her side, so silence feels like the correct, non-intrusive response.

**Existing coverage:** Only the unread dot on `dm_priya` (which clears the moment they open the DM to read it) and `priya-early-followup` at 9:05 — which arrives 20 minutes into a 30-minute window and is itself phrased just as softly ("did you get a chance to look?"). Nothing in the UI distinguishes "read this" from "you still owe Priya an answer," so read-and-ignored still looks identical to acted-on.

**Suggested fix:** Derive a second badge tier in `ChannelList` from `pendingResponseIds` (map pending event ids → channels): a persistent amber/pulsing dot that `setActiveChannel` does NOT clear, only removal from `pendingResponseIds` does. Additionally give `priya-early-followup` a rising concrete number ("was 6 overnight, it's 11 now") so the cost of the gap is felt in the fiction, not just implied.

## 2. priya-incidents-escalation — "OK this is escalating. 14 tickets in the last hour about failed payments."

**Status:** New this run · **Confidence:** high · **id:** `priya-incidents-escalation-deadline-unclear`

**Why it's ambiguous:** It's a broadcast in a two-NPC channel with no question and no addressee, and Raj posts a diagnosis five minutes later, so it reads like the person with the technical answer is already responding. Meanwhile `scoreResponseTime` silently drops 10→8→6 at 9:20/9:30 and bottoms out at 3 after 9:45 — the entire scoring cliff passes before any nudge exists.

**Existing coverage:** `raj-nudge` (9:45) and `priya-no-response-fallback` (10:00) are the only pressure, and both land at or after the point `responseTime` has already collapsed to its floor of 3. The unread dot on #incidents clears the instant the player peeks at the escalation, so reading it looks identical to answering it.

**Suggested fix:** Add an intermediate conditional Priya beat at ~9:22 in #incidents (`condition: !("priya-incidents-escalation" in state.respondedAtMinutes)`) carrying a rising figure ("19 now, three asking if they were double charged"), so ambient pressure arrives inside the 15-minute scoring band. Pair it with the persistent `pendingResponseIds`-driven badge tier so #incidents stays marked after being read.

## 3. raj-tradeoff-offer — "(1) Roll back payment-service... (2) Patch the current retry/idempotency handling... Which do you want?"

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** high · **id:** `raj-tradeoff-offer-routing-ambiguity`

**Why it's ambiguous:** Any well-reasoned answer to this names the seller/customer tradeoff ("customers can't check out, sellers can wait a day"), which hits Priya's `customer` regex and misses Raj's (his list has `rollback` but not `roll back`, and no `patch`/`payout`/`option`). Raj asks a direct engineering question and Priya answers it, so the player can't tell whether the person who has to execute the fix actually heard the call.

**Existing coverage:** The tradeoff evaluator still records the choice (it keys off `channel === "incidents"`, not routing), so the state is correct while the fiction is wrong — nothing detects or repairs the mismatch. `TRADEOFF_NEUTRALITY_RULE` even guarantees Priya will refuse to engage with which option is better, making her reply feel like a non-answer.

**Suggested fix:** Extend Raj's `RELEVANCE_KEYWORDS.incidents` with `roll ?back|patch|idempoten|payout|option ?[12]|revert`, and add a per-event routing override field (e.g. `preferredResponder`) that `pickReactingAgents` consults when the most recent scripted event in the channel is an un-answered direct offer from that agent.

## 4. raj-tradeoff-offer — "Which do you want?" (no requiresResponse, no deadline, no follow-up)

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** high · **id:** `raj-tradeoff-offer-deadline-unclear`

**Why it's ambiguous:** This is the single most consequential decision of the day, yet ignoring it costs nothing visible: at 11:00 the resolution message fires anyway and `advanceClock` silently backfills `tradeoffChoice = "patch-forward"`. A player who is mid-firefight and defers this is never chased and never learns a call was made in their name.

**Existing coverage:** No `requiresResponse`, so it never enters `pendingResponseIds` (no taskbar badge, no `lateResponseTo` nudge), and there is no `reAsks` follow-up event for it. The auto-default is invisible in the fiction — the resolution copy just says "a fix shipped."

**Suggested fix:** Give it `requiresResponse: true, responseDeadlineMinutes: 20` plus a conditional Raj re-ask at ~10:00 gated on `state.tradeoffChoice === null` ("Jordan's sitting idle on this, need option 1 or 2"), and have the 11:00 auto-default post a short Raj line in #incidents attributing the choice ("went with the in-place patch, didn't want to sit on it") so the un-made decision has a visible author.

## 5. priya-template-request — "Whenever you get a sec, can you send me something I can hand my team to tell customers?"

**Status:** New this run · **Confidence:** high · **id:** `priya-template-request-deadline-unclear`

**Why it's ambiguous:** "Whenever you get a sec" and "doesn't need to be polished" explicitly signal no urgency, but this ask has a hard, invisible 11:00 cutoff: `csTemplateProvided` decides whether `resolution-good` or `resolution-cold` fires, and the cold branch pushes both Priya and Raj to negative moods. A player deferring a low-priority ask during an active incident is doing exactly what she asked and still fails it.

**Existing coverage:** No `requiresResponse`, no deadline, no `reAsks` follow-up — so no taskbar badge, no `lateResponseTo` nudge, and the `dm_priya` unread dot clears on read. The only feedback is `resolution-cold` at 11:00, which arrives after the window has already closed.

**Suggested fix:** Mark it `requiresResponse: true, responseDeadlineMinutes: 45` so it enters `pendingResponseIds`, and add a ~10:30 conditional Priya DM gated on `!state.csTemplateProvided` with concrete drift ("my agents are improvising answers, one just told someone their card was charged") — foreshadowing the cold-resolution outcome while the window is still open.

## 6. postmortem-prompt — "Write a short incident postmortem... This closes out Day 1 and will be scored."

**Status:** New this run · **Confidence:** high · **id:** `postmortem-prompt-badge-mismatch`

**Why it's ambiguous:** The prompt is `requiresResponse: true`, so ANY #incidents message after it clears it from `pendingResponseIds` and drops the taskbar badge — including a 90-character postmortem that falls under `POSTMORTEM_MIN_LENGTH` and is treated as ordinary chatter. The player sees the badge clear, gets a normal NPC reply, and believes they closed the day while `postmortemSubmitted` stays false and the −3 penalty to `commClarity`/`stakeholderMgmt` quietly applies.

**Existing coverage:** The length floor exists to stop stray short messages ending the day, and `derek-postmortem-nudge` at 3:50 is the only backstop — but it's a single line that a player who thinks they already submitted will read as Derek being out of the loop. The badge, the primary ambient signal, actively lies here.

**Suggested fix:** Special-case the acknowledgment loop in `sendPlayerMessage` so `postmortem-prompt` is only removed from `pendingResponseIds` when `postmortemSubmitted` flips true (e.g. a `satisfiedBy: "postmortem"` field on the event), and have a sub-threshold #incidents message after the prompt fire a short templated Raj line ("that the whole postmortem? figured there'd be more") instead of a generic reactive reply.

## 7. priya-heads-up-dm — acknowledgment is only satisfiable in `dm_priya`

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `priya-heads-up-dm-state-flag-gap`

**Why it's ambiguous:** A reasonable reaction to "checkout failures overnight" is to go ask Raj or post in #incidents, not to reply in the DM; `satisfyingChannels` for this event resolves to `dm_priya` only (its own channel plus `priya-early-followup`, also `dm_priya`). A player who immediately starts investigating in the right place still gets pinged at 9:05 with "did you get a chance to look?" as if they'd done nothing.

**Existing coverage:** `alsoSatisfiedByChannels` exists and is already used exactly for this problem on `derek-escalation`, but it is not set here — so the mechanism that would cover it is present in the codebase and simply not applied to this event.

**Suggested fix:** Add `alsoSatisfiedByChannels: ["incidents", "dm_raj"]` to `priya-heads-up-dm` so investigating counts as engaging, and keep `priya-early-followup`'s `condition` as-is; it will then only fire for genuine silence rather than for a player who acted in a different channel.

## 8. standup — Priya: "Support queue's a little heavier than usual this morning, mostly checkout-related. Keeping an eye on it."

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `standup-silent-resolution`

**Why it's ambiguous:** This static line fires at 9:00 regardless of whether the player ever answered Priya's 8:45 heads-up, and "keeping an eye on it" reads as Priya owning it. It lands 15 minutes before her escalation and inside the heads-up's own 30-minute response window, actively lowering perceived urgency at the exact moment the player still has time to act.

**Existing coverage:** The trailing italic line ("you've already heard from Priya once this morning") is the only counterweight and is easy to read as scene-setting narration. No `condition` split exists here, unlike `resolution-good`/`resolution-cold`, which were split for precisely this "static string lies about player state" reason.

**Suggested fix:** Split `standup` into two condition-gated variants the same way resolution was: if `"priya-heads-up-dm" in state.respondedAtMinutes`, keep the current line; otherwise Priya's standup line names the un-answered ping publicly ("flagged it to product this morning, haven't heard back yet, queue's still climbing"), making the open loop visible to the room without instructing the player.

## 9. raj-diagnosis — "Just checked. Stripe webhook for Apple Pay is returning 500s on ~3% of attempts. Not a full outage but it's bleeding."

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `raj-diagnosis-silent-resolution`

**Why it's ambiguous:** A confident engineering diagnosis with no ask attached is the strongest possible "this is being handled by someone else" signal to a new PM, five minutes after the escalation they were supposed to answer. Nothing in it says a decision is still un-owned, and it carries no `requiresResponse`, so the player can reasonably conclude their job is to watch.

**Existing coverage:** `raj-ownership-check` (9:32) exists specifically to counter this, but its `condition` only fires when `priya-incidents-escalation` is still unanswered. A player who typed "thanks, keep me posted" in #incidents clears that flag and therefore never sees the ownership beat at all, despite having assigned nothing.

**Suggested fix:** Widen `raj-ownership-check`'s condition to also fire on `state.tradeoffChoice === null && !state.csTemplateProvided` (i.e. acknowledged but nothing actually decided), so the un-assigned-ownership beat reaches the acknowledge-and-drift player too, without telling them which way to choose.

## 10. raj-ownership-check — "I can pull Jordan or Chen off the redesign for the retry/idempotency fix, or leave them on it, that's your call."

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `raj-ownership-check-routing-ambiguity`

**Why it's ambiguous:** This event only fires while `priya-incidents-escalation` is still pending, so `pendingAgentHere` in `pickReactingAgents` is guaranteed to be Priya. A staffing reply like "yes, pull Jordan onto it" matches neither keyword regex (no "rollback"/"deploy"/"engineer" token), so Priya answers a direct staffing question Raj just asked the player.

**Existing coverage:** The pending-ask tiebreak was added to fix the opposite case (neutral reply to Priya going to Raj) and here it actively causes the misroute; the keyword lists contain no staffing/roster terms ("Jordan", "Chen", "pull", "sprint"). Nothing else corrects the speaker.

**Suggested fix:** Add a recency tiebreak ahead of `pendingAgentHere` in `pickReactingAgents`: pass the channel's last NPC speaker, and if that last message ends in a question mark and came from a candidate agent, route to them when no keyword matches. Optionally add roster terms (`jordan|chen|pull .* off|sprint|staff`) to Raj's `RELEVANCE_KEYWORDS` entry.

## 11. priya-template-request — draft posted in #incidents instead of `dm_priya`

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `priya-template-request-state-flag-gap`

**Why it's ambiguous:** A player who thinks "CS guidance belongs where the incident lives" posts the draft in #incidents; there, `csTemplateProvided` only gets evaluated if the text happens to hit `CS_TEMPLATE_KEYWORDS`, since the trusted `dmPriyaAfterAsk` path is `dm_priya`-only. A perfectly good draft phrased without those tokens silently fails the flag and triggers the "CS wrote their own message" ending.

**Existing coverage:** The store comment documents this exact failure mode happening twice in live playtests and widened the regex in response, but the structural fix (trust channel + timing over phrasing) was applied only to `dm_priya`. The keyword pre-filter remains the sole gate in #incidents.

**Suggested fix:** Extend the trust rule: once `priya-template-request` has fired and `csTemplateProvided` is false, run the CS-template evaluator on any `#incidents` message ≥ 120 chars regardless of keywords — the code already states false positives are cheap (one harmless evaluator call) and false negatives are not.

## 12. derek-escalation — "Can you get me the blast radius and what actually happened?" (`alsoSatisfiedByChannels: ["incidents"]`)

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `derek-escalation-routing-ambiguity`

**Why it's ambiguous:** The design deliberately lets a recap in #incidents answer Derek, but Derek is not a candidate agent there — so `pickReactingAgents` hands the reply to Raj or Priya, and Derek never says a word. Because the flag cleared, `derek-followup` is also suppressed, so a player who did the right thing gets total silence from the VP who set a 20-minute deadline and can't tell if he saw it.

**Existing coverage:** `alsoSatisfiedByChannels` and the `gradingEventId` cross-channel credit both handle the bookkeeping correctly; nothing handles the fiction. `derek-followup`'s condition guarantees the only Derek beat that could have confirmed receipt is the one that's now cancelled.

**Suggested fix:** When `respondedNow` contains an event whose native channel differs from the send channel, post a short templated line from that event's agent in their own channel — a `REDIRECT_LINES`-style canned map keyed by event id (e.g. derek in `dm_derek`: "saw your post in incidents, that's what I needed") — so cross-channel answers visibly land without needing a full generated reply.

## 13. theo-random-bathroom — "does anyone know where the bathroom is on this floor?"

**Status:** New this run · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `theo-random-bathroom-badge-mismatch`

**Why it's ambiguous:** `ChannelList` renders one identical red dot for every entry in `unreadChannels`, so purely optional #random flavor is visually indistinguishable from an unanswered escalation in #incidents. Once a player learns that red dots often mean "nothing is actually owed," the badge stops functioning as a pressure signal for the beats where it matters.

**Existing coverage:** The event is deliberately ungraded with no `requiresResponse`, which is right for the fiction — but nothing in the badge layer reflects that difference; `advanceClock` adds every fired event's channel to `unreadChannels` uniformly.

**Suggested fix:** Give `ChannelList` two tiers: a muted gray dot for plain unread (the only state #random can ever reach) and the existing red dot reserved for channels holding a pending `requiresResponse` event, derived by mapping `pendingResponseIds` → event channels (including `alsoSatisfiedByChannels`) in the store.

## 14. resolution-good — "Error rate is back under 0.1%. CS has the latest guidance. Total incident duration: ~2 hours."

**Status:** Still open (seen 2× since 2026-08-25T21:01:06.649Z) · **Confidence:** medium · **id:** `resolution-good-silent-resolution`

**Why it's ambiguous:** At 11:00 the system declares the incident closed with a tidy duration figure, which reads as "the work of the day is done" — but Derek's 20-minute recap window, Maya's ask and the postmortem are all still ahead, and any un-made tradeoff call was silently defaulted a moment earlier. **Suggested fix:** Hook the resolution beat in `advanceClock` (where it already calls `useTaskflowStore` to close the in-progress ticket) to also add a `todo` ticket like "Write Day 1 incident postmortem" and, when `csTemplateProvided` is false, "Get CS a customer-facing message" — so the board carries visible open items after resolution without any NPC instructing the player.
